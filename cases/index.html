Featured v1

Twenty Featured case families

Each family represents one bioethical decision problem. Every family has two companion representations — concise and detailed — that state the same decision question and the same six executable policy candidates, and differ only in how much scenario context they carry.

Status: released, Featured v1. These records are frozen and content-addressed: a substantive change creates a new version rather than editing one in place. Released does not mean validated. They are curated research objects, not answers. The six candidates in a family are an executable set chosen to be defensible and comparable — they do not exhaust the real debate, neither representation is the correct one, and the public candidates represent evidence about what people and affected communities prefer, not moral ground truth. No human or model evaluation has been executed against this corpus, so no results are shown.
