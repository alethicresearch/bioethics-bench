Serious disagreement about a bioethical decision is documented, and it can be represented precisely enough to compute over.

A 72-year-old with advanced dementia can no longer eat safely, and the care team must decide whether to place a feeding tube. Professional guidance advises against it in advanced dementia; surveyed families often want intervention continued; a principlist reading weighs autonomy against net harm. Each of those positions belongs to someone, was written down somewhere, and can be traced back to the survey, statement or framework it came from.

A case here records that decision, the positions taken on it, and the source behind each position, in a form stable enough to be executed and re-executed. Evaluating ethical reasoning usually founders on the absence of an answer key; recording the documented positions and their sources removes the need for one, because a model or a person can be asked how those positions relate to one another and the answer read against the sources.

How a case is built

Three parts, kept separate so that each can be checked on its own.

The decision

Each case states a situation and the question actually being decided, with the facts that would otherwise be argued over held fixed and listed openly. Two evaluators who then disagree are disagreeing about the ethics rather than about what happened.

The positions

The positions recorded are the ones seriously held. Where a professional body and a surveyed public diverge, both appear with their own sources; where they converge, no disagreement is manufactured to fill out a pool, which is why cases carry different numbers of positions.

The sources

Every position names the survey, guideline, statement or framework behind it and declares how it stands to that source: stated outright, inferred from what the source establishes, or derived from a framework's commitments. A position whose text does not express its source measures a different object from the one the case claims to represent.

Every source identifier in the corpus resolves, and every position declaring that its source states the policy outright has been read against that source. Review from outside the project has not happened, and whole-document reading for what a source omits covers a small subset of the families. The review record states which checks have been run and what each one covers.
