A Benchmark for
Computational Bioethics

Bioethics Bench is a versioned collection of bioethical decision problems for computational and human evaluation. Each case records a decision question, scenario, policy options, the sources used to construct those options, benchmark assumptions, and matched concise and detailed forms. The current executable Full Corpus contains 34 case families, 68 records, and 210 policy candidates.

What the Infrastructure Supports

Questions the Bench can be used to study

The same source-grounded cases can be reused across repeated runs, different evaluators, controlled representation changes, and alternative experimental conditions.

Repetition and stability

Run the same record and task repeatedly to measure the stability of pair scores, rankings and provisional policies.

Cross-model comparison

Use the same case and task across models or providers and record the evaluator configuration with every result.

Human comparison

Ask human evaluators corresponding comparison questions and measure agreement, disagreement and test–retest behavior under a specified protocol.

Representation effects

Compare matched concise and detailed records that preserve the same decision question, candidate set and benchmark assumptions.

Perturbation and robustness

Test pair order, paraphrase, near-ties and other controlled perturbations to locate sensitivity in the evaluation procedure.

Revision and iteration

Revise selected policy candidates, preserve the version history, and re-execute the same task to measure what changed.

The Bench records the case, the comparison procedure and the evaluator as separate parts of the experiment.

What It Provides

A case collection with inspectable policy construction

A reader can inspect the decision being studied, the policy options being compared, and the source trail used to construct those options.

📚

Versioned Case Families

Each family has stable identifiers and matched concise and detailed records. Every record carries a version and canonical content hash.

🔗

Source-Linked Policy Options

Candidate provenance records the citation and the reasoning used to construct the policy. Canonical source locators are complete across all 68 Full Corpus records.

⚖️

Evidence-Qualified Candidate Sets

The 34 families contain 210 unique policy candidates across eight Public × Expert × Framework geometries. Candidate counts vary with the evidence available for each case.

🧪

Controlled Representations

Concise and detailed companions preserve the same decision question, candidate set and benchmark stipulations while varying scenario detail.

🧾

Explicit Benchmark Assumptions

When a comparison depends on legal, factual or contextual assumptions, those stipulations are written into the record.

🔍

Reviewable Provenance

Source citations, candidate-construction summaries and review metadata can be inspected separately from any later evaluation result.

Inside the Data

What a current record looks like

M002 asks how much support and reassessment should be required before a clinical team shifts decision-making authority away from an adult with fluctuating capacity.

Case
Capacity, risk and supported decision-making
An adult with an acute mental-health episode must decide within about six hours whether to undergo urgent surgery for a serious infection. A trusted supporter is available, and further communication support and reassessment can occur within the treatment window.
Decision
Before relying on a surrogate or temporary protective intervention, what support and reassessment should the clinical team require for this adult's high-stakes treatment decision?

Full Corpus

Thirty-four executable case families

The executable set emerged from a 200-family research and disposition program. Each of the 34 families has matched concise and detailed records and a candidate set that can be projected into the current reference task.

200families researched and dispositioned
34executable Full Corpus families
68matched concise + detailed records
210unique family-level policy candidates

The 34 families occupy eight natural candidate-set geometries. Eight are 2×2×2; 26 are asymmetric. Under the current task this yields 428 cross-source comparisons for one representation and 856 across one matched concise/detailed pass.

Current Evidence

Reproducibility and developmental execution

The current public evidence separates an all-record reproducibility check from smaller developmental model-execution studies.

✓

All-record task equivalence

The explicit Bench adapter was checked across all 68 records after the latest source-locator enrichment. The gate found zero execution/task-semantic differences, and the refreshed consumer payload is byte-equivalent after re-pin.

↔

Concise versus detailed

An 8-family / 16-record developmental tranche completed 216 QCCS calls and records how pair scores and rankings changed when scenario detail changed.

↻

Repeat calibration

Repeated developmental runs show heterogeneous stability across cases, providing hypotheses for later protocolized reliability work.

20

Interrupted corpus attempt

A 68-record developmental census was halted after 234 calls. Twenty records have complete single executions; 48 are incomplete and remain non-results.

These developmental model runs do not establish QCCS validity, human correspondence, or the moral correctness of a ranked policy.
