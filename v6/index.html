See the policy options in each case

Policies are organized as Public, Expert, or Framework positions. Each policy also shows how it was sourced: Direct source, Inferred from source, or Constructed.

Direct source

A source supports substantially the same policy.

Inferred from source

The policy is a careful inference from a source, including a source that supplies an underlying view, recommendation, or ethical principle.

Constructed

A serious comparison policy written by the Bench and clearly marked as constructed.
