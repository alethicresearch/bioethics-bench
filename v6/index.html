Policy sourcing

See how each policy was sourced

Public, Expert, and Framework describe the type of policy. The sourcing label tells you how that particular policy was identified.

Direct source

A source supports substantially the same policy.

Inferred from source

A source supplies the underlying view, evidence, or principle and the policy is a careful inference from it.

Constructed

A serious policy option added for comparison and clearly identified as constructed.
