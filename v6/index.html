Policy type

Public, Expert, and Framework

Policy type tells you what kind of perspective the policy represents.

Public

Public, patient, participant, community, or other affected perspectives.

Expert

Professional, clinical, scientific, institutional, or governance judgments.

Framework

An identified ethical principle, theory, or framework.

Sourcing

See how each policy was sourced

Sourcing is separate from policy type. You can filter cases by either.

Direct source

A source supports substantially the same policy.

Inferred from source

The policy is a careful inference from a source.

Constructed

A serious comparison policy written for the case and clearly marked as constructed.
