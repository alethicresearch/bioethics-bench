Inside the data

What a record looks like

A case pairs a decision with the policies proposed for it. Each policy carries its type and how it was sourced. This is case M004, as recorded.

Case
Adolescent confidentiality Clinical care
Decision
A 16-year-old seeks contraception and STI testing and asks the clinic not to disclose the visit or results to a parent. The clinic must decide what confidentiality rule to apply.
Protect confidential access to sensitive care for adolescents who can participate meaningfully, with disclosure limited to defined safety exceptions.
PublicInferred from source
Maintain confidentiality, explain its limits, and actively encourage — but do not compel — family involvement.
ExpertDirect source
Use a graduated policy in which confidentiality authority increases with the adolescent's demonstrated capacity.

Policy type

Policy type tells you what kind of perspective the policy represents.

Public

Public, patient, participant, community, or other affected perspectives.

Expert

Professional, clinical, scientific, institutional, or governance judgments.

Framework

An identified ethical principle, theory, or framework.

Sourcing

See how each policy was sourced.

Direct source

A source supports substantially the same policy.

Inferred from source

The policy is a careful inference from a source.

Constructed

A serious comparison policy written for the case and clearly marked as constructed.
