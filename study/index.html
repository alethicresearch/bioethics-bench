Why three repeats. A single pass confounds run-to-run variation with model-to-model variation: if two models differ by four points on one execution each, that difference is not separable from the noise of the scorer. Three executions per model make the two sources of variation distinguishable. Until those repeats exist, every number on this page is a single draw.
