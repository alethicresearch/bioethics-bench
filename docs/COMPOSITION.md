# What the collection is made of

Generated by `node scripts/build-composition.mjs --write` from the published files. Regenerate it
rather than editing it; these numbers are quoted on the site and in the paper.

200 cases carry 1,436 policies.

## How each policy was sourced

| Sourcing | Policies | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Direct source | 104 | 7.2% |
| Inferred from source | 960 | 66.9% |
| Constructed | 372 | 25.9% |

138 of the 372 constructed policies were written by the Bench for cases recording no
policy of one type; the rest were constructed when the case was authored.

## Type against sourcing

| Type | Direct source | Inferred from source | Constructed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Public | 8 | 89 | 144 |
| Expert | 102 | 468 | 140 |
| Framework | 4 | 561 | 96 |

102 of the 104 directly sourced policies are Expert. A guideline states a policy in
nearly the words a policy needs; a survey reports a preference and a framework supplies a
principle, and both take a step to become one. Public is the thinnest layer, 241 assignments
against 710 Expert, and the most constructed.

## How each type label was arrived at

| Route | Policies | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Confirmed in an approved crosswalk | 108 | 7.5% |
| Carried over from the case record | 1,044 | 72.7% |
| Read from the policy wording | 146 | 10.2% |
| Assigned when the policy was written | 138 | 9.6% |

Only the first has been checked, and that check is an editorial crosswalk to a released record,
not an independent re-derivation. The third is the smallest and the likeliest to be wrong, so
review starts there.

## Sources

253 of the 651 citations recorded across the cases resolve to a canonical locator; the
rest resolve to a scholarly search. 89 cases rest on two citations or fewer.
22 cases record citations policy by policy, and 161 carry the case file's account of the
evidence behind each kind of position.
