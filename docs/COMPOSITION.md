# What the corpus is made of

Generated by `node scripts/build-composition.mjs --write` from the published files. Regenerate it
rather than editing it; the numbers here are quoted on the site and in the paper.

200 cases carry 1436 policies.

## How each policy was sourced

| Sourcing | Policies | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Direct source | 104 | 7.2% |
| Inferred from source | 960 | 66.9% |
| Constructed | 372 | 25.9% |

138 of the 372 constructed policies were written by the Bench for a case that
recorded no policy of one of the three types; the rest were constructed when the case was authored.

## Type against sourcing

| Type | Direct source | Inferred from source | Constructed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Public | 8 | 89 | 144 |
| Expert | 102 | 468 | 140 |
| Framework | 4 | 561 | 96 |

Direct sourcing is almost entirely an Expert phenomenon: 102 of the 104 directly
sourced policies are Expert. A professional guideline states a policy in nearly the words a policy
needs; a survey reports a preference and a framework supplies a principle, and both take a step to
become one. The Public layer is the thinnest — 241 assignments against
710 Expert — and the most constructed.

## How each type label was arrived at

| Route | Policies | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Confirmed in an approved crosswalk | 108 | 7.5% |
| Carried over from the case record | 1044 | 72.7% |
| Read from the policy wording | 146 | 10.2% |
| Assigned when the policy was written | 138 | 9.6% |

Only the first has been checked against a source, and that check is an approved editorial crosswalk
to a released record rather than an independent re-derivation. The third is the smallest and the
likeliest to be wrong, which is where review is worth spending first.

## Sources

662 citations are recorded across the 200 cases, 253 of them resolving to a
canonical locator and the rest to a scholarly search. 89 cases rest on two citations or
fewer. 22 cases record citations policy by policy;
161 carry the case file's own account of the evidence behind each kind of position.
