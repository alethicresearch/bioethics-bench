## 28. [article] Informed Consent for Ambient Documentation Using Generative AI in Ambulatory Care. JAMA Netw Open. 2025. Observational quality-improvement study at NYU Langone Health, March-December 2024; 121 participants (18 ambulatory clinicians, 103 patients). 74.8% of patients reported comfort with ambient documentation; 81.6% consented when given basic information, falling to 55.3% when given detailed information about AI features, data storage and corporate involvement. Comfort varied by encounter type: 63.1% for routine physical examination, 40.8% for sexual health, 35.0% for mental health. Over 98% rated knowing where audio was sent and who could access recordings as important. The authors recommend opt-in consent with a flexible multimodal approach including previsit notification, in-visit confirmation, nonclinical staff support and opt-out options.
