## What changed from v4

V5 moves the manuscript from architecture/evidence integration into **manuscript-grade source integration**.

The Source Grounding section now demonstrates the construct-validity argument through specific corpus cases rather than generic statements:

- **M002 / WHO QualityRights:** an earlier candidate used a real, correctly identified source but encoded the `last resort` framing that the QualityRights/CRPD-aligned position rejects. The current candidate and provenance explicitly record the repair.
- **M031 / Oregon Death with Dignity:** the prior source was a legitimate Oregon Health Authority annual data report, but that report did not state the statutory safeguards encoded by the candidate. Warrant was repaired to ORS 127.800–127.897; the data summary remains evidence about program operation rather than the source of the policy.
- **M041 / ESHRE IVF allocation:** passage-level support for a minimum-effectiveness threshold was genuine, but whole-document review found the same paper also gives priority to a childless couple when only one can be treated. The current record declares that omission rather than treating the candidate as a complete representation of ESHRE on the family's prior-children axis.
- **M028 / brain-death accommodation:** direct-policy evidence is illustrated by the difference between the survey's institutional-policy question (41.9% agreeing hospitals should be required to continue treatment after family rejection) and its separate self-preference question (24.4%). The Bench uses the policy-targeted item.
- **M060 / source independence:** Public candidates are now grounded in population evidence (including Dahl 2006, Winkelman 2015, Kippen 2018), while Expert candidates remain grounded in the 2022 ASRM Ethics Committee opinion, avoiding nominally cross-source cells that compare a professional source with itself.
