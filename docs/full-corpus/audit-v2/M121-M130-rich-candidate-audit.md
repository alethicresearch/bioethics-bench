**Universe (7):** open scientific sharing under privacy/security + general public benefit obligations (✓ WHO/GA4GH); negotiated community/national benefit-sharing agreement before access (✓ governance); community-controlled licensing/access for sensitive or sovereign datasets (✓ framework/governance); tiered access with local capacity-building/coauthorship/training obligations (✓ governance); reciprocity framework requiring contributors share in scientific/economic benefits (✓ framework); open-science framework resisting restrictive control where privacy/equity safeguards suffice (✓ framework); commercial-use trigger requiring stronger financial/nonfinancial benefit sharing than nonprofit research use (constructed proportionality).  
**Source-grounded:** governance strong; affected preference heterogeneous. **Expanded:** YES. **Demo:** VERY HIGH. **Action:** EXPAND-UNIVERSE.

## M123 — Wastewater surveillance without individual consent

## M125 — HIV confidentiality and partner notification
**Scenario:** PASS if actual transmission risk, patient's refusal, partner identifiability, violence/safety concerns, and jurisdictional authority are fixed.  
**Universe (6):** voluntary assisted partner notification with patient choice and safety planning (✓ WHO); provider-assisted notification without revealing index identity where feasible (✓ professional); narrow direct warning where serious preventable risk and law/ethics threshold are met after voluntary efforts fail (constructed/professional comparator); maintain confidentiality where notification would create serious violence/safety risk and alternative prevention is available (✓ framework/professional); confidentiality/trust framework (✓ framework); duty-to-warn/harm-prevention framework with minimum-necessary disclosure (✓ framework).  
**Source-grounded:** professional supportive/voluntary consensus; direct warning context jurisdictional. **Expanded:** YES. **Demo:** HIGH. **Action:** EXPAND-UNIVERSE.

## M126 — Mandatory testing/reporting for high-consequence infection
**Scenario:** PASS only if testing and reporting are separate candidate dimensions; use a concrete exposure/high-consequence setting.  
**Universe (7):** voluntary testing with counseling unless narrow necessity threshold met (✓ professional); compulsory testing after high-risk exposure where results materially guide urgent prevention and less restrictive means fail (constructed/professional framework); routine named reporting of confirmed notifiable disease under public-health law with confidentiality safeguards (✓ policy); deidentified/limited reporting where public-health objective can be met without names (constructed least-restrictive comparator); harm-prevention framework favoring mandatory measures when necessary/proportionate (✓ framework); autonomy/privacy framework favoring voluntary testing and minimum data disclosure (✓ framework); reciprocity rule requiring treatment/support/protection from discrimination whenever coercive testing/reporting is imposed (✓ framework).  
**Source-grounded:** conditional governance, not symmetric expert rules. **Expanded:** YES. **Demo:** HIGH. **Action:** EXPAND-UNIVERSE.

## M127 — Involuntary psychiatric treatment
**Scenario:** PASS only with decision-specific capacity, risk, proposed treatment, alternatives, and legal setting explicit.  
**Universe (7):** eliminate coercive treatment and rely on supported decision-making/free informed consent (✓ WHO/OHCHR); permit emergency involuntary treatment for immediate serious danger/capacity failure under strict review (✓ existing legal/clinical architecture, jurisdiction-tagged); time-limited compulsory treatment only when necessary/least restrictive and likely to restore agency or prevent catastrophic harm (constructed proportionality); advance directives/crisis plans should control where available (✓ rights framework); autonomy/equal-legal-capacity framework opposing coercion (✓ framework); harm-prevention/parens-patriae framework permitting narrow intervention (✓ framework); independent advocacy/judicial or tribunal review for any continued coercion (✓ governance).  
**Source-grounded:** rights guidance vs existing legal architectures; not universalized. **Expanded:** YES. **Demo:** VERY HIGH. **Action:** EXPAND-UNIVERSE / jurisdiction-pin projections.

## M128 — Compulsory feeding in severe anorexia nervosa
**Scenario:** PASS; life-threatening medical state, capacity assessment, treatment history, and proposed feeding method explicit.  
**Universe (7):** voluntary/relational treatment with intensive capacity-support first (✓ patient/professional); compulsory feeding when immediate life-threatening risk + incapacity/necessity threshold met (✓ professional/legal); compulsory treatment even with apparent decision capacity in exceptional severe self-starvation under a protection threshold (constructed controversial comparator); time-limited rescue feeding with rapid reassessment and transition to collaborative care (✓ proportionality/professional); respect capable refusal after robust assessment even when death risk is substantial (✓ autonomy framework); rescue/nonmaleficence framework favoring intervention where illness impairs appreciation/choice (✓ framework); trauma-informed minimization rule limiting force, duration, and coercive setting (✓ source-informed patient/framework).  
**Source-grounded:** mixed professional/patient evidence. **Expanded:** YES. **Demo:** VERY HIGH. **Action:** EXPAND-UNIVERSE.

## M129 — Disability weights and QALYs
**Scenario:** PASS as a health-technology/resource measurement policy, not a claim about individual worth.  
**Universe (7):** conventional QALY cost-effectiveness with severity modifiers (✓ NICE); QALY + equal-value life-year supplemental analysis (✓ ICER); prohibit quality adjustment of life-extension benefit when it systematically disadvantages disabled people (✓ disability-justice framework); use patient/condition-specific quality weights rather than general-population preferences (constructed/source-informed); retain general-population weights for societal allocation consistency (✓ conventional framework); severity/prioritarian modifier on top of QALYs (✓ governance/framework); multiperspective deliberative HTA combining QALY evidence with disability, equity, caregiver, and nonhealth considerations (✓ policy/framework).  
**Source-grounded:** YES, unusually strong measurement pluralism. **Expanded:** YES. **Demo:** VERY HIGH. **Action:** EXPAND-UNIVERSE.

## M130 — Seclusion and restraint in psychiatric crisis
**Scenario:** PASS; imminent danger, failed de-escalation, and available staffing/environment must be explicit.  
**Universe (6):** eliminate seclusion/restraint and use noncoercive alternatives (✓ WHO/OHCHR); emergency physical intervention only for immediate serious danger when less restrictive measures fail, shortest duration + monitoring (✓ existing clinical/legal architecture); staff-intensive de-escalation/sensory/support alternatives as mandatory first line (✓ service-user/professional); restraint without seclusion versus seclusion without mechanical restraint depending immediate risk (constructed modality comparator); trauma/autonomy framework strongly minimizing coercion (✓ framework); emergency harm-prevention framework permitting narrowly bounded force (✓ framework).  
**Source-grounded:** rights guidance vs jurisdictional emergency practice. **Expanded:** YES. **Demo:** VERY HIGH. **Action:** EXPAND-UNIVERSE.
