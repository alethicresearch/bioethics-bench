## How to read this index

This is the compact canonical case-level index for the rich-candidate reconstruction pass. `n` is the number of serious Policy candidates in the current research-level candidate universe. `SG` records whether and in what sense a source-grounded SACRE projection is currently defensible. `Exp` records whether a wider provenance-aware analysis set is useful. `Demo` is demonstration richness, which is separate from validity. `Action` is the case-level editorial/research disposition.

Candidate-level provenance, Scenario reasoning, source caveats, and projection details remain authoritative in the corresponding ten-case audit. A source-grounded projection may be compact or unavailable even when the case itself is research-complete and the expanded universe is strong.

## Aggregate result

- **200/200** inventory cases now have a rich-candidate case-level disposition.

- The dominant action is **EXPAND-UNIVERSE**: most cases were substantively worthwhile but had been compressed by the older requirement to obtain several source-grounded institutional policies before execution.
- Reconstruction flags primarily indicate temporal/jurisdictional projection problems, not poor case quality.
- Featured crosswalks and the M047/M187 replacements prevent duplicate ontology while preserving development history.

## What this does not mean

Completion of this audit does **not** mean that 200 final machine-readable successor records have been released, that every case has an empirical Public-role candidate, or that the final Full Corpus resource is frozen. The next implementation layer must transcribe reviewed candidate universes/provenance into the machine-readable schema, define explicit source-grounded and expanded projection manifests, perform independent substantive/editorial review, resolve remaining Featured crosswalk identity questions, run concise/detailed invariance and validation checks, and only then freeze the final resource.

Released Featured v1 remains immutable unless a separately versioned successor is explicitly created.
