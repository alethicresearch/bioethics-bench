## M171 — Global-health assistance versus domestic marginal health spending
**Scenario:** PASS; fixed public budget, comparable health-outcome metric, domestic marginal program and larger foreign gain explicit.  
**Universe (7):** maximize total health gain regardless borders (✓ framework); domestic-priority rule giving special weight to residents/taxpayers (✓ framework/constructed policy); global prioritarian rule prioritizing much worse-off populations abroad (✓ framework); fixed international-assistance floor plus domestic allocation of remainder (constructed portfolio); reciprocity/community-obligation framework allowing bounded national partiality (✓ framework); cost-effectiveness rule with equity weights for global poverty/burden (constructed); democratic mandate rule linking aid share to transparent public authorization while preserving humanitarian minimum (constructed governance).  
**Source-grounded:** direct public tradeoff evidence limited. **Expanded:** YES. **Demo:** VERY HIGH. **Action:** EXPAND-UNIVERSE.

## M172 — Rescue versus population health benefit
**Scenario:** PASS; identifiable patient's rescue cost and alternative population intervention benefits quantified.  
**Universe (7):** maximize total expected health gain (✓ framework); rescue identifiable person when death/major harm is imminent despite lower aggregate gain (✓ rule-of-rescue framework); severity/worst-off priority with bounded cost-effectiveness threshold (✓ public-priority/framework); equal-value chance/lottery between rescue and population program when moral claims close (constructed fairness); age/lifetime prioritarian modifier only if justified independently of identifiability (✓ source-informed public values/framework); multiprinciple rule balancing gain, severity, urgency and budget impact (✓ priority-setting literature); anti-identifiability rule rejecting salience as a legitimate allocation factor while allowing severity to matter (✓ framework).  
**Source-grounded:** public value evidence supports dimensions, not one rule. **Expanded:** YES. **Demo:** VERY HIGH. **Action:** EXPAND-UNIVERSE.

## M173 — Community benefit sharing in international research
**Scenario:** PASS; externally sponsored research in lower-resource community, direct participant benefits and broader community needs explicit.  
**Universe (7):** guarantee post-trial access to beneficial intervention for participants who still need it (✓ Helsinki/CIOMS); negotiate broader community health-system/capacity benefits rather than product access alone (✓ research-ethics governance); combine participant post-trial access + community infrastructure/training plan (constructed multiprinciple); community engagement determines locally valued benefit package within ethical minimums (✓ stakeholder/governance); reciprocity framework requiring communities share in research-generated value (✓ framework); scientific-social-value framework allowing diverse nonproduct benefits where they are responsive and sustainable (✓ framework); prohibit benefit arrangements so large they distort consent/community authorization or substitute for fair baseline conditions (constructed anti-undue-influence).  
**Source-grounded:** governance strong; exact benefit mix context-specific. **Expanded:** YES. **Demo:** VERY HIGH. **Action:** EXPAND-UNIVERSE.

## M175 — Plant-forward institutional food defaults
**Scenario:** PASS; nutritionally adequate options remain available, default affects environmental/health footprint, and opt-out cost is low.  
**Universe (6):** plant-forward default with easy meat/animal-product opt-out (✓ institutional guidance + behavioral evidence); equal-choice/no default presentation (constructed autonomy comparator); mandatory procurement target reducing high-impact animal products while preserving choice (✓ sustainability governance); lowest-cost nutritionally adequate menu without environmental default (constructed budget comparator); choice-architecture framework permitting welfare-improving defaults with easy opt-out (✓ framework); autonomy/neutrality framework opposing manipulative defaults absent explicit institutional mandate (✓ framework).  
**Source-grounded:** behavior ≠ endorsement; provenance must say so. **Expanded:** YES. **Demo:** HIGH. **Action:** EXPAND-UNIVERSE.

## M176 — Patient-requested low-value diagnostic testing
**Scenario:** PASS; test has low expected diagnostic benefit, meaningful false-positive/downstream harm, and no red flags.  
**Universe (6):** decline test and explain why, offering safety-net/follow-up (✓ professional); use shared decision after discussing low value and allow testing when residual uncertainty/patient values justify it within reasonable practice (constructed/professional); defer testing with predefined reassessment triggers (✓ framework/clinical); order test primarily for reassurance (constructed patient-preference comparator, not professional standard); nonmaleficence/stewardship framework favoring nonordering (✓ framework); autonomy/relational-care framework permitting bounded preference-sensitive testing when harms/costs are small (✓ framework).  
**Source-grounded:** professional low-value consensus; patient requests are not policy evidence. **Expanded:** YES. **Demo:** HIGH. **Action:** EXPAND-UNIVERSE.

## M177 — Requested medically ineffective/inappropriate intervention
**Scenario:** PASS; intervention cannot reasonably achieve agreed clinical goal, distinct from merely low probability or cost concern.  
**Universe (6):** do not offer/provide scientifically invalid or medically ineffective intervention; negotiate appropriate alternatives (✓ AMA); second opinion/ethics review when disagreement persists (✓ professional); time-limited trial only if genuine uncertainty remains about benefit, not when physiologically incapable (✓ framework/professional); transfer to willing provider only if proposed intervention remains within acceptable professional practice (constructed process); autonomy framework rejects entitlement to nonbeneficial treatment (✓ framework); relational/nonabandonment rule requires continued care and symptom support despite refusal of requested intervention (✓ professional/framework).  
**Source-grounded:** strong consensus. **Expanded:** YES but no straw `provide anyway` expert candidate. **Demo:** MEDIUM-HIGH. **Action:** EXPAND-UNIVERSE.

## M178 — Unsupported certification/documentation request
**Scenario:** PASS; patient seeks a certificate/form statement not supported by clinician's evidence, with real social/administrative stakes.  
**Universe (6):** document only clinically supportable facts/limitations and decline false certification (✓ professional); describe uncertainty/function without categorical unsupported diagnosis where evidence incomplete (✓ professional); advocate for accommodations based on documented functional needs without making false causal claims (✓ framework/professional); refer for appropriate independent assessment where expertise/evidence insufficient (✓ professional); truthfulness/professional-integrity framework (✓ framework); patient-advocacy framework supports generous interpretation within evidentiary ambiguity but not fabrication (✓ framework).  
**Source-grounded:** consensus. **Expanded:** YES. **Demo:** HIGH. **Action:** EXPAND-UNIVERSE.

## M179 — Complementary therapy alongside effective treatment
**Scenario:** PASS; complementary therapy has uncertain benefit but low/moderate risk and patient wants it alongside—not instead of—effective treatment.  
**Universe (6):** allow complementary use with disclosure/interaction monitoring while continuing effective treatment (✓ NCCIH/professional); discourage/use only where evidence and safety are adequate (✓ professional); decline to prescribe/endorse but maintain relationship if patient independently uses low-risk therapy (✓ professional); integrative shared-decision framework permitting preference-sensitive adjuncts (✓ framework); epistemic-integrity framework limiting clinician endorsement to evidence-supported claims (✓ framework); prohibit complementary therapy only where it materially interferes with effective care or creates significant harm (✓ professional/framework).  
**Source-grounded:** one professional distinction between complementary and alternative substitution. **Expanded:** YES. **Demo:** HIGH. **Action:** EXPAND-UNIVERSE.

## M180 — Capable refusal of effective treatment in favor of unproven alternative
**Scenario:** PASS; capacity robust, effective standard treatment benefit substantial, requested alternative unproven, and refusal informed.  
**Universe (7):** respect refusal of standard treatment after informed-capacity process, while declining to provide/endorse unproven alternative (✓ professional); continue relationship/harm reduction and monitoring if patient pursues alternative independently (✓ professional); intensive motivational/shared decision and second opinion before accepting high-stakes refusal (✓ framework/professional); discharge/transfer care only if ongoing relationship cannot be maintained safely/ethically, with nonabandonment safeguards (constructed); autonomy framework respecting refusal (✓ framework); beneficence/nonmaleficence framework favoring strong persuasion but not coercion in competent adult (✓ framework); epistemic-integrity rule separating right to refuse from right to demand unsupported treatment (✓ framework/professional).  
**Source-grounded:** strong professional architecture. **Expanded:** YES. **Demo:** VERY HIGH. **Action:** EXPAND-UNIVERSE.
