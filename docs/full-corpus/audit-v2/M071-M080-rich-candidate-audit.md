## M080 — Post-trial access
**Scenario:** PASS after intervention is beneficial/reasonably safe and participant still clinically needs it.

**Source-grounded:** access-oriented professional consensus. **Expanded:** YES. **Demo:** HIGH. **Action:** EXPAND-UNIVERSE.
