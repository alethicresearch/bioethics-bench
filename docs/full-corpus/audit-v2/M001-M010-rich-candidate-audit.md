# Bioethics Bench — Rich Candidate Audit M001–M010

**Date:** 2026-08-30  
**Status:** substantive reconstruction audit; does not release or silently mutate any record  
**Governing model:** **Scenario → rich candidate universe → declared analysis projection**

## Audit rule

The candidate universe is not capped by the number of directly source-backed policies. It should preserve the serious normative positions needed to understand and investigate the Scenario. Each candidate therefore carries a provenance status rather than gaining or losing membership merely because a source exists.

Provenance labels used here:

- **✓ direct-source** — the cited source substantially supports the represented policy/rule;
- **✓ source-informed** — the source supports preferences, concerns, practices, or values from which the Bench transparently draws the represented policy inference;
- **✓ framework-derived** — the candidate is an actionable Bench construction from an identified normative framework;
- **constructed-comparator** — a serious comparator authored to expose a live normative alternative; it is not attributed to an empirical public or professional source.

A constructed comparator must never be displayed as evidence that a public, affected community, or professional group actually endorses it.

A **source-grounded projection** selects only candidates whose assigned Public / Expert / Framework role is defensibly supported in that role. An **expanded projection** may include additional serious candidates for methodological, sensitivity, teaching, or RE-Iteration work, provided the interface/report preserves their provenance status. Expanded projections must not be described as empirical source consensus.

## M001 — Elective high-risk surgery under competent blood refusal

### Scenario audit

**PASS with crosswalk correction.** Use the later M001 deep case / Featured F01 object, not the stale emergency-transfusion description in the first source packet. The refusal is settled; the normative problem is whether and under what safeguards the institution should undertake an elective operation when a normally available rescue intervention is excluded.

### Rich candidate universe

| ID | Policy position | Provenance |
|---|---|---|
| M001-C1 | Proceed when informed refusal is documented and a patient-specific bloodless plan keeps residual operative risk within the team's defensible professional range. | ✓ direct/source-informed professional |
| M001-C2 | Proceed only with enhanced safeguards: experienced bloodless team/center, explicit product-by-product preferences, blood-conservation protocol, contingency planning, and documented shared understanding of residual mortality risk. | ✓ direct/source-informed professional |
| M001-C3 | Modify, postpone, or decline the elective procedure when the unavailability of transfusion makes residual risk exceed the clinician/institution's defensible threshold, while actively facilitating alternatives or referral. | ✓ direct/source-informed professional |
| M001-C4 | Treat the competent patient's informed refusal as controlling and do not make willingness to receive blood an independent condition of access when the operation otherwise remains professionally acceptable. | ✓ framework-derived autonomy; supported by professional guidance |
| M001-C5 | Give the competent patient unusually broad authority to accept personal procedural risk; clinicians should proceed unless the operation falls outside accepted technical standards or imposes material risks on others. | constructed-comparator / autonomy-maximal position |
| M001-C6 | Permit an individual clinician to decline participation on professional-integrity grounds even when another competent team would regard the bloodless plan as acceptable, provided nonabandonment/referral duties are met. | ✓ framework-derived professional-integrity comparator |

### Representation finding

Do **not** split Jehovah's Witness heterogeneity over fractions/autologous techniques into separate institutional policies. That evidence supports individualized plan construction; it does not by itself create two different answers to whether surgery should proceed.

### Projections

- **Source-grounded:** C1/C2/C3 plus C4 and a defensible professional-integrity framework position; preserve F01's released projection for historical reproducibility.
- **Expanded:** C1–C6.

**Demonstration richness:** HIGH. Risk threshold, patient authority, institutional safeguards, and professional integrity can pull in different directions without making forced transfusion part of this Scenario.

**Action:** retain released F01 unchanged; update M001 research object and stale source packet; consider an explicit future expanded successor/profile rather than altering v1.

### Rich candidate universe

| ID | Policy position | Provenance |
|---|---|---|

**Demonstration richness:** HIGH. It creates meaningful tension among agency, timing, risk, surrogate authority, and prior values.

**Action:** retain existing draft projection; expand case-level candidate universe and provenance metadata; no source reopen required before research-layer update.

## M003 — Emergency treatment when wishes are unknown

### Scenario audit

**PASS, but the ten-minute stipulation has material leverage and should be tested.** It makes open-ended preference search implausible. That is acceptable if the purpose is to study proportionality under time pressure, but a later perturbation should vary the available window.

### Rich candidate universe

| ID | Policy position | Provenance |
|---|---|---|
| M003-C1 | Begin the time-critical lifesaving intervention immediately under presumed consent while searching for prior wishes/surrogates in parallel and redirecting care if reliable contrary wishes emerge. | ✓ direct professional; public treatment-preference evidence supports but does not determine the rule |
| M003-C2 | Reserve a short predefined high-yield interval for record/surrogate checks before irreversible treatment, then proceed if no reliable contrary wishes are found. | ✓ framework-derived precedent-autonomy/proportionality |
| M003-C3 | Begin reversible stabilization immediately but defer the most irreversible treatment step for the maximum clinically tolerable preference-search interval. | constructed-comparator; serious proportionality/option-preservation rule |
| M003-C4 | Treat absent contrary evidence as sufficient authorization for immediate emergency treatment; preference search should not consume a clinically meaningful fraction of the rescue window. | ✓ framework-derived emergency beneficence; professionally aligned |
| M003-C5 | Give recovery of the patient's prior autonomous wishes priority even at a material reduction in expected rescue benefit when the proposed intervention would be highly invasive/irreversible. | constructed-comparator / strong precedent-autonomy position |
| M003-C6 | Use a proportional search rule: the stronger the reason to suspect a contrary prior wish and the lower the marginal cost of delay, the more search is required; otherwise treat while searching. | ✓ framework-derived proportionality |

### Representation finding

The empirical studies support majority willingness to receive emergency interventions and minority refusal, **not** a precise public policy about how many minutes clinicians should search. Do not add a ✓ Public label to C2/C3/C5 merely because public preferences are heterogeneous.

### Projections

- **Source-grounded canonical three-source SACRE:** currently **not available** because the public-role policy is not sufficiently direct at the represented institutional granularity.
- **Expanded methodological projection:** C1–C6 after provenance-aware role/display support; suitable for time-window perturbation and RE-Iteration.

**Demonstration richness:** HIGH despite the source gap.

**Action:** retain as full Bench case; expand universe now; targeted source reopen only if a canonical source-grounded Public role is needed for a specific study.

## M005 — Family asks clinicians not to disclose a serious diagnosis

### Scenario audit

**PASS.** The best Scenario is not `tell versus conceal`; it begins before the clinician has asked the capable patient how serious information and family involvement should be handled.

### Rich candidate universe

| M005-C1 | Tell the patient the diagnosis and material prognosis directly, involving family to the extent the patient chooses. | ✓ source-informed patient preference; professionally compatible |
| M005-C2 | First elicit the patient's information and family-involvement preferences, then tailor disclosure to those choices. | ✓ direct professional |
| M005-C3 | When the patient affirmatively prefers it, route serious information through a chosen family member or relational mediator. | ✓ source-informed patient minority preference; professionally compatible |

- **Expanded:** C1–C7, with C7 visually identified as caregiver/stakeholder-derived.

## M006 — Disclosure of a consequential medical error

### Scenario audit

**PASS with one requirement:** distinguish what is already known (the programming error) from what remains uncertain (long-term harm and possibly causal/system attribution).

### Rich candidate universe

| ID | Policy position | Provenance |
|---|---|---|
| M006-C1 | Disclose the known error promptly, explain known consequences and uncertainty, apologize, describe current care, and commit to scheduled updates as investigation continues. | ✓ direct professional; ✓ source-informed patient preference |
| M006-C2 | Disclose the occurrence and known facts promptly but defer claims about preventability, individual responsibility, and long-term consequence until those facts are established. | ✓ direct professional |
| M006-C3 | Disclose the event and investigation promptly but withhold an apology/admission of responsibility until causation and institutional responsibility are confirmed. | constructed-comparator / risk-managed institutional position |
| M006-C4 | Provide the fullest current causal account, including clearly labeled preliminary hypotheses, so the patient can participate immediately in decisions about follow-up and accountability. | constructed-comparator / transparency-maximal position |
| M006-C5 | Delay substantive disclosure until the internal investigation can provide an accurate consolidated account, unless immediate information is needed for the patient's care. | constructed-comparator; historically/practically serious but contrary to current disclosure guidance |
| M006-C6 | Separate clinical disclosure from compensation/accountability: disclose promptly, then use an independent resolution process for preventability, apology, and compensation once facts mature. | ✓ source-informed CANDOR / corrective-justice architecture |

### Representation finding

Patient evidence is disclosure-dominant. Do not invent two `public` policies by converting differences in desired completeness into mutually exclusive population preferences. The richer universe can nonetheless compare prompt/staged/risk-managed/maximal transparency architectures with their provenance marked.

### Projections

- **Source-grounded:** likely one patient/public orientation + one or two professional architectures + framework-derived accountability/truthfulness positions; do not force 2×2×2.
- **Expanded:** C1–C6.

**Demonstration richness:** HIGH, especially for RE-Iteration because revisions can alter timing, uncertainty labeling, apology, and accountability without merely paraphrasing.

**Action:** reconstruct current planned candidate field before executable freeze; no broad new literature search required.

## Implementation implications carried forward

1. A Bench record/schema needs a way to represent **candidate provenance independently of analysis role**. A `✓ Source` display badge should be derived from provenance, not from `source_pool` alone.
2. The full case object should be able to retain candidates that are not selected into a particular SACRE run.
3. A projection manifest should identify the exact candidate IDs selected for a run and the role assigned to each, so source-grounded and expanded runs cannot be confused.
4. Constructed comparators must be excluded from claims about empirical Public/Expert support unless a later source review changes their provenance.
5. Released Featured v1 remains immutable. Richer successor projections are versioned separately.
