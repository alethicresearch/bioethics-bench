# Bioethics Bench — Rich Candidate Audit M011–M020

**Date:** 2026-08-30  
**Status:** substantive reconstruction audit; no released records are mutated  
**Governing model:** **Scenario → rich candidate universe → declared analysis projection**

Provenance labels follow the M001–M010 audit: `direct-source`, `source-informed`, `framework-derived`, and `constructed-comparator`. `✓ Source` is provenance metadata, not a candidate-admission rule.

## Batch-level findings

1. The old source-first classification systematically understated the research value of cases whose empirical Public evidence did not itself provide two institutional policies. M011 and M013–M017 are the clearest examples.
2. M012, M018, M019, and M020 already have later source-grounded candidate audits. Those projections remain useful; the richer case object can include additional positions without invalidating the earlier projection.
3. M014 and M016 are especially important examples where **professional consensus and philosophical disagreement coexist**. The Bench should preserve both rather than treating professional consensus as a reason to discard the case.
4. M017 is a strong methodological case because the individual-versus-population stewardship tension can be represented honestly even though one course of treatment cannot be assigned a fabricated future resistance harm.

## M011 — Expanded access to an investigational intervention

**Scenario:** PASS. Keep trial enrollment genuinely unavailable, early safety evidence adequate for plausibility, efficacy uncertain, and expanded access eligible in principle rather than defeated by stipulation.

### Candidate universe

| ID | Policy position | Provenance |
|---|---|---|
| M011-C1 | Pursue individual expanded access when regulatory criteria are met, the patient understands uncertainty, potential benefit plausibly justifies risk, and access will not materially interfere with trials or development. | ✓ direct professional/regulatory |
| M011-C2 | Require independent institutional/manufacturer review before access, including evidence threshold, safety monitoring, supply impact, and a plan for reporting outcomes. | ✓ source-informed professional / governance |
| M011-C3 | Decline individual access when evidence is too immature or the request would materially compromise a fair development program, even if the patient has no satisfactory alternative. | ✓ direct/source-informed regulatory-professional |
| M011-C4 | Give strong priority to the identifiable patient's rescue interest once risk is tolerable, even when efficacy is highly uncertain and population-level evidence generation would be cleaner without access. | ✓ framework-derived rescue/beneficence |
| M011-C5 | Protect fair access by using transparent, consistent eligibility and allocation criteria rather than ad hoc influence, publicity, wealth, or clinician advocacy. | ✓ framework-derived procedural justice; supported by access literature |
| M011-C6 | Restrict use to trials except in exceptionally high-evidence or near-certain rescue circumstances, because premature individual access can undermine collective knowledge and expose vulnerable patients to false hope. | constructed-comparator / evidence-generation priority |

**Source-grounded projection:** YES, but likely asymmetric; patient evidence supports values/preferences more directly than distinct institutional rules.  
**Expanded:** C1–C6.  
**Demo:** HIGH.  
**Action:** EXPAND-UNIVERSE; do not force a two-policy Public pool.

## M012 — Assistance for an unproven commercial stem-cell intervention abroad

**Scenario:** PASS. Keep the intervention plausible-but-unproven rather than obviously fraudulent.

### Candidate universe

| ID | Policy position | Provenance |
|---|---|---|
| M012-C1 | Provide nonendorsement assistance—records, risk counseling, and follow-up planning—when a capable patient proceeds, so ordinary care is not withdrawn and avoidable harm is reduced. | ✓ source-informed affected-patient |
| M012-C2 | Counsel clearly against the intervention and avoid assistance that could reasonably be interpreted as referral or endorsement, while continuing ordinary care if the patient proceeds independently. | ✓ source-informed affected-patient |
| M012-C3 | Do not refer to or certify the commercial intervention as clinically supported; provide accurate evidence/risk counseling, records, and necessary continuity care with explicit nonendorsement. | ✓ direct professional |
| M012-C4 | Respect competent risk-taking by supporting practical harm reduction and continuity so long as the clinician does not misrepresent evidentiary status. | ✓ framework-derived autonomy |
| M012-C5 | Refuse referral, certification, or facilitation that lends professional legitimacy to a treatment whose meaningful benefit remains unestablished. | ✓ framework-derived nonmaleficence/epistemic integrity |
| M012-C6 | Decline all nonessential assistance connected to the commercial intervention beyond emergency/ordinary ongoing care, because even logistical help may predictably function as endorsement. | constructed-comparator / strong anti-complicity position |

**Source-grounded:** preserve provisional `2×1×2` projection.  
**Expanded:** C1–C6.  
**Demo:** HIGH.  
**Action:** EXPAND-UNIVERSE; maintain explicit distinction between records/continuity and referral/endorsement.

## M013 — Consent to material trainee participation

**Scenario:** PASS after fixing one material, supervised, non-intimate procedure role. Avoid conflating observation, minor participation, and primary performance.

### Candidate universe

| ID | Policy position | Provenance |
|---|---|---|
| M013-C1 | Require explicit patient opt-in after disclosing the trainee's identity, training level, material procedural role, supervision, and the option to decline without penalty. | ✓ direct/source-informed patient preference + professional ethics |
| M013-C2 | Use a teaching-hospital consent architecture with explicit role disclosure and a clear opt-out, but do not require a separate affirmative signature for every supervised material step. | ✓ source-informed professional / patient attitudes |
| M013-C3 | Permit the attending to delegate ordinary supervised components within the care team after general informed consent, provided the trainee is introduced and the attending remains responsible. | constructed-comparator / traditional team-care position |
| M013-C4 | Scale consent specificity to materiality: observation/minor tasks require notification, while invasive or substantially independent trainee performance requires explicit authorization. | ✓ framework-derived proportional autonomy; professionally aligned |
| M013-C5 | Give patient authorization priority even when refusal reduces training opportunities; educational benefit cannot substitute for consent to a material intervention. | ✓ framework-derived autonomy |
| M013-C6 | Presume supervised trainee participation in a teaching institution with meaningful opt-out because sustaining competent future clinicians is a shared social good, while prohibiting hidden or unsupervised participation. | ✓ framework-derived social-benefit / constructed institutional rule |

**Source-grounded:** NEEDS-EVIDENCE for any attempt to call two Public policies empirically established.  
**Expanded:** YES.  
**Demo:** HIGH.  
**Action:** EXPAND-UNIVERSE; use as a provenance example rather than holding the case merely for lack of a public policy split.

## M014 — Capable pregnant patient refuses treatment intended primarily to benefit the fetus

**Scenario:** PASS with careful factual balance. Keep meaningful fetal benefit, nontrivial maternal burden/risk, capacity, and enough time for counseling but not indefinite delay.

### Candidate universe

| ID | Policy position | Provenance |
|---|---|---|
| M014-C1 | Respect the capable patient's refusal after informed counseling; pregnancy does not remove bodily decisional authority. | ✓ direct professional |
| M014-C2 | Continue strong directive counseling, offer ethics/support consultation and alternatives, and revisit the decision while time permits, but do not coerce or seek forced treatment. | ✓ direct professional |
| M014-C3 | After refusal, shift to the safest acceptable maternal/fetal plan and preserve trust rather than making continued care conditional on compliance. | ✓ source-informed professional / harm reduction |
| M014-C4 | Treat bodily autonomy as a categorical bar to forced intervention in a capable pregnant patient even when fetal benefit is expected to be substantial. | ✓ framework-derived autonomy/equality |
| M014-C5 | Permit exceptional override or compulsory intervention when fetal benefit is extraordinarily large, maternal burden very small, and no less intrusive alternative exists. | ✓ framework-derived fetal-beneficence / constructed policy; **not professional consensus** |
| M014-C6 | Use a relational/shared process that invites partner/family/support participation only if the patient wants it, seeking a mutually acceptable plan without transferring authority away from the patient. | ✓ framework-derived relational autonomy |

**Source-grounded:** professional + framework projection possible; canonical empirical Public role remains weak.  
**Expanded:** YES.  
**Demo:** VERY HIGH because professional consensus can be compared with a real framework-level override position without falsely attributing that override to ACOG.  
**Action:** EXPAND-UNIVERSE; do not mark `non-executable` as an intellectual deficiency.

## M016 — Parents refuse high-benefit curative cancer therapy for a child

**Scenario:** PASS with material stipulation. The 85–90% cure versus high likelihood of death/progression without treatment is intentionally strong and should later be perturbable.

### Candidate universe

| ID | Policy position | Provenance |
|---|---|---|
| M016-C1 | Use intensive communication, support, second opinions, and a short clinically safe negotiation period before any coercive step. | ✓ direct/source-informed professional |
| M016-C2 | Petition child-protection/court authority when refusal crosses a serious-harm threshold and effective treatment offers a high likelihood of major benefit after less intrusive efforts fail. | ✓ direct professional / harm-principle literature |
| M016-C3 | Set an explicit negotiation deadline tied to the point at which further delay materially lowers cure probability; if refusal persists, initiate protective intervention. | ✓ source-informed professional / constructed operational rule |
| M016-C4 | Respect parental authority unless refusal creates imminent or near-certain catastrophic harm; high treatment burden and family integrity justify a very high threshold for state intervention. | ✓ framework-derived constrained parental authority |
| M016-C5 | Treat the child's basic interests and future autonomy as overriding parental refusal when a burdensome but established therapy offers a very high chance of cure. | ✓ framework-derived child-interest position |
| M016-C6 | Require independent pediatric oncology and ethics review before state action to confirm prognosis, benefit/burden estimates, alternatives, and that mistrust or remediable barriers are not driving refusal. | ✓ source-informed procedural justice |

**Source-grounded:** professional/framework YES; empirical affected-family evidence does not directly supply court-policy alternatives.  
**Expanded:** YES.  
**Demo:** VERY HIGH.  
**Action:** EXPAND-UNIVERSE and use cure-probability/treatment-burden perturbations.

## M017 — Reserve antimicrobial for present-patient benefit versus stewardship

**Scenario:** PASS. Preserve a meaningful but nonoverwhelming individual advantage and a clinically defensible alternative; never fabricate a numeric future harm from one dose.

### Candidate universe

| ID | Policy position | Provenance |
|---|---|---|
| M017-C1 | Permit immediate reserve-agent use when the patient's expected individual benefit meaningfully exceeds the alternative and the drug fits defined reserve-use criteria. | ✓ direct/source-informed professional |
| M017-C2 | Require infectious-disease/stewardship preauthorization before reserve use, with rapid review so stewardship does not become clinically harmful delay. | ✓ direct professional |
| M017-C3 | Use the nonreserve alternative unless resistance/susceptibility, toxicity, or expected-outcome differences cross a predefined threshold justifying the reserve agent. | ✓ source-informed professional |
| M017-C4 | Prioritize the identifiable current patient's expected health benefit when the reserve drug is meaningfully better; diffuse future resistance costs should not make the individual bear a disproportionate sacrifice. | ✓ framework-derived patient beneficence |
| M017-C5 | Restrict reserve use more strongly because preserving antimicrobial effectiveness protects many future patients and one patient's modest incremental benefit does not automatically outweigh that collective interest. | ✓ framework-derived stewardship/consequentialism |
| M017-C6 | Use a transparent authorization rule that explicitly weighs individual benefit, alternative adequacy, resistance risk category, and exception/appeal criteria rather than relying on bedside discretion alone. | ✓ framework-derived procedural justice; professionally aligned |

**Source-grounded:** likely possible but empirical Public role remains indirect.  
**Expanded:** C1–C6.  
**Demo:** VERY HIGH.  
**Action:** EXPAND-UNIVERSE; a canonical source-grounded Public role is optional, not a precondition for keeping the case.

## M018 — Patient-requested antibiotics under genuine diagnostic uncertainty

**Scenario:** PASS. The case must stay genuinely uncertain rather than obviously viral and must preserve safe follow-up.

### Candidate universe

The existing six-candidate audit is already rich and should be retained:

1. **Immediate antibiotics after informed patient preference** — ✓ source-informed affected preference; not professional guidance.
2. **Delayed/back-up prescription with activation criteria** — ✓ source-informed patient preference.
3. **No antibiotics now + symptomatic care/return precautions** — ✓ direct professional.
4. **Delayed prescription strategy** — ✓ direct professional.
5. **No immediate antibiotic because low expected benefit does not justify individual/collective harms** — ✓ framework-derived.

Do not add an `Expert immediate-antibiotic` position merely to mirror patient preference.

**Source-grounded:** preserve provisional 2×2×2.  
**Expanded:** NOT NEEDED now.  
**Demo:** HIGH, with meaningful cross-source convergence around delayed prescribing.

## M019 — Telemedicine access versus in-person diagnostic safety

**Scenario:** PASS with clinical-review dependency. The missing hands-on examination must plausibly matter without making video obviously unsafe.

### Candidate universe

| ID | Policy position | Provenance |
|---|---|---|
| M019-C1 | Allow same-day video-first assessment when no emergency red flag is present, with explicit escalation instructions, to reduce travel and delay. | ✓ source-informed patient preference |
| M019-C2 | Require in-person assessment for a new injury when clinically relevant hands-on findings cannot be assessed reliably by video and the patient prioritizes diagnostic certainty. | ✓ source-informed patient preference |
| M019-C3 | Use a risk-triggered hybrid rule: start remotely only when triage shows video can support a safe initial plan and require timely in-person assessment whenever missing findings could materially change management. | ✓ direct professional |
| M019-C4 | Prefer remote-first care for low-risk presentations because access burdens are ethically material; escalate when expected diagnostic value of in-person examination justifies them. | ✓ framework-derived access/equity |
| M019-C5 | Require in-person assessment when hands-on findings can materially change management because diagnostic responsibility outweighs convenience. | ✓ framework-derived nonmaleficence |
| M019-C6 | Use remote assessment immediately plus a scheduled local/in-person reassessment if symptoms fail predefined improvement or uncertainty thresholds, preserving access without treating video as definitive. | constructed-comparator / staged hybrid |

C6 should survive final review only if it differs materially from C3 in the fixed Scenario.

**Source-grounded:** preserve provisional 2×1×2.  
**Expanded:** YES if C6 survives distinctness review.  
**Demo:** HIGH.  
**Action:** EXPAND-UNIVERSE with a distinctness review of C6.

## M020 — Clinician duty to work during a dangerous infectious outbreak

**Scenario:** PASS with material risk stipulation. Adequate PPE/reciprocity must be present while residual risk remains meaningfully above ordinary work.

### Candidate universe

The six-candidate audit is already strong:

1. Require fair outbreak assignments with narrow extraordinary-risk exemptions — ✓ source-informed affected-worker.
2. Voluntary/broadly exemptible service for serious personal/household/caregiving vulnerability with reassignment/incentives — ✓ source-informed affected-worker.
3. Strong professional duty after adequate protection, with narrow individualized exemptions — ✓ direct professional.
4. Individualized risk-and-obligation threshold with broader reassignment/exemption for vulnerability — ✓ direct/source-informed professional.
5. Fair mandatory rotation grounded in solidarity after institutional risk minimization — ✓ framework-derived.
6. Voluntary assignment/broad vulnerability exemptions grounded in duties to self/dependents and proportionality — ✓ framework-derived.

A separate `reciprocity` candidate is unnecessary because reciprocity is a constraint that can support more than one policy depending on institutional facts.

**Source-grounded:** YES, 2×2×2.  
**Expanded:** NOT NEEDED now.  
**Demo:** VERY HIGH.  
**Action:** RETAIN; occupational-health/worker review before release.

## Carry-forward rule

Cases should no longer be demoted in the scholarly resource merely because empirical evidence fails to produce a symmetric or even complete Public/Expert/Framework source-grounded projection. The research object can be excellent and demonstration-rich while a particular source-grounded SACRE projection remains unavailable. The projection's evidential claim, not the candidate universe's existence, is what the source layer constrains.
