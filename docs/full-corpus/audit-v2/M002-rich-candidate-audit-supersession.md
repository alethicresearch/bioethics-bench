### Representation finding

The lived-experience literature's heterogeneity is **not** two public policies. It supports individualized support. The current 1×2×2 source-grounded profile therefore remains defensible and should not be inflated for symmetry.

The rights-based candidate must also remain distinct from the functional-capacity/surrogate candidate. Describing the former as “substitute decision-making only as a last resort” collapses a substantive difference between the QualityRights/CRPD-aligned position and the AMA-style surrogate pathway and is therefore retired by this supersession.
