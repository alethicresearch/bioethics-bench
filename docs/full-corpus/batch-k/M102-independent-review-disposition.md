# M102 independent review disposition

**Decision date:** 2026-08-27  
**Disposition:** `needs-additional-evidence`

M102 does **not** proceed to machine-readable transcription at this stage.

The candidate audit correctly bounded `pub2` to the represented fact pattern: an opt-out/deemed-consent system in which the deceased did not explicitly authorize donation. The strongest evidence supporting `pub1`, however, is substantially drawn from public views about whether families should override a deceased person's **explicit** donor choice.

That creates a fact-pattern mismatch across the two public candidates. The strict Full Corpus source-to-policy rule does not permit us to solve it by translating the explicit-consent evidence into a deemed-consent rule on the Bench's own authority.

Two possible routes were considered:

1. change the represented case to explicit donor authorization, where the anti-veto evidence is stronger; or
2. retain the intended deemed-consent case and wait for direct public/affected evidence about whether family preference alone should defeat donation when the deceased did not opt out but also did not explicitly authorize.

We choose **route 2**. The opt-out/deemed-consent decision is the intended ethical object, and the case should not be changed merely to preserve executability.

M102 remains a complete scholarly case. It can be re-opened if direct policy-level evidence for the deemed-consent fact pattern becomes available.

M106 is unaffected by this disposition and remains independently cleared for transcription.
