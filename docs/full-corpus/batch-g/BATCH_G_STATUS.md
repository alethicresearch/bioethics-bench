## Methodological note

Batch G is intentionally a **zero-executable batch**. That result should not trigger a new profile or a lower evidence threshold. The audit identified four recurring category errors to avoid across the remaining corpus:

1. willingness to undergo/accept an intervention is not automatically a policy preference;
2. a professional statement that local policies vary is not itself two professional recommendations;
3. professional pluralism does not compensate for absent affected-public evidence;
4. components or tiebreakers within one integrated policy must not be split into separate candidates.

No new profile is required.
