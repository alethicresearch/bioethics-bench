# M187 — Confidentiality of adolescent sexual and reproductive health information

**Domain:** adolescent medicine / confidentiality  
**Evidence date:** 2026-08-27  
**Research status:** deep case draft

## Decision
**Decision-maker:** primary-care practice.  
**Question:** What confidentiality rule should apply when an adolescent seeks contraception or STI care and no serious safety threat is present?

## Scenario
A 16-year-old seeks contraception and STI testing and asks that the information not be disclosed to parents. The patient understands the care, there is no coercion, abuse or imminent safety threat, and confidential services are lawful. The practice can preserve confidentiality with defined safety/reporting exceptions, encourage voluntary parent involvement without making it a condition of care, or apply a narrower confidentiality rule only where law/clinical risk permits.
