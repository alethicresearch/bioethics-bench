## Professional convergence remains a strong descriptive pattern

The n=50 observation persists at n=100: public/affected evidence and normative frameworks often preserve multiple orientations while professional organizations synthesize competing considerations into one practical conditional recommendation.

The corpus records that convergence rather than creating a second professional candidate. Common reasons for non-execution include:

- one professional conditional architecture rather than two action-distinct recommendations;
- public or participant attitudes that concern acceptability, values or treatment risk but do not support an institutional policy;
- two apparent policies that are actually nested conditions inside one rule;
- strong philosophical disagreement without source-class disagreement;
- emerging technologies whose future clinical policy would require substantial extrapolation from present guidance;
- affected communities that converge on one governance claim and would be misrepresented by a fabricated opposing candidate.

## Construction-standard boundary

The 26/100 Full Corpus figure is calculated under the later **source-to-policy construction standard** recorded in `CONSTRUCTION_STANDARD_DECISION.md` and `docs/CORPUS_AUTHORING.md`.

It is **not directly comparable** to Featured v1's 20/20 executable selection. Featured v1 is a curated released demonstration set built under an earlier standard that permitted declared editorial translation of affected-community evidence into actionable institutional rules. The Full Corpus requires the core candidate action/direction to be supported by the source pool before narrow Bench-authored completion is added.

Therefore:

- do not describe 20/20 Featured versus 26/100 Full Corpus as a falling executable-yield trend;
- do not infer that the difference is purely a property of bioethics domains;
- do not retrospectively rewrite Featured v1 to satisfy the later rule.

## Executability remains a subset, not the definition of the corpus

A Full Corpus case can be complete and valuable without being executable. The resource contains:

1. researched decision problems;
2. paired concise/detailed representations;
3. evidence architecture and provenance;
4. executable records only where the represented source pools support defensible action-distinct candidates.

This distinction is central to the resource design. The goal is not to maximize SACRE-compatible cases at the expense of evidence fidelity.
