# M025 author revision after independent review

**Status:** author-side repair proposal only; **does not clear M025**.  
**Trigger:** independent review found a scenario/action-divergence failure in `pub2`.

## Review finding

The represented parents already decline cochlear implantation because they prioritize Deaf identity and immediate fluent sign-language access. The old `pub2` said:

> Favor timely cochlear implantation for an eligible young child **when the family prioritizes spoken-language and auditory opportunities**, while maintaining sign-language and developmental support.

On the represented facts, that antecedent is false. `pub2` therefore issues no contrary instruction and the public pool does not span the action axis in practice.

## Narrow proposed repair

Replace only `pub2` with:

> **pub2 — early auditory-opportunity pathway:** Recommend timely cochlear implantation for an eligible young child in order to preserve auditory-language opportunity that may diminish with delay, while maintaining immediate fluent sign-language access and developmental support and stopping short of coercive override while the child has an adequate language pathway.

The intended first action on the represented scenario is therefore: **recommend proceeding with timely implantation despite the parents' present preference to defer, while preserving sign-language access and respecting the legal boundary against coercive override on the stipulated facts.**

No change is proposed to `pub1`, `exp1`, `fw1`, `fw2`, or to the concise/detailed scenario facts.

## Why this is a repair rather than a new candidate

The source packet already identifies an affected-parent orientation that places substantial weight on early implantation, spoken-language/auditory opportunity and the timing-sensitive benefits of implantation. The old wording accidentally made that orientation conditional on the represented family already sharing it. The revision removes that self-defeating antecedent; it does not add a new rationale or a new policy axis.

Primary affected anchors remain the same:

- parental decision-making regarding cochlear implantation, PMID 15236889;
- parental values in cochlear-implant decisions, PMID 11006449;
- parent/Deaf-community perspectives on cochlear implants, PMID 15148196;
- Deaf-parent decision-making evidence, PMID 21941879.

Professional timing/candidacy evidence remains the 2022 American Cochlear Implant Alliance pediatric guideline, PMID 35213891, and belongs to `exp1`, not to the provenance of `pub2`.

## Re-review questions

The independent reviewer should reapply all four gates rather than simply confirm the wording change:

1. **Source-to-policy fidelity:** do the affected-parent sources support the core action `recommend timely implantation`, or do they support only parents' own choice/preferences? If the latter, M025 must remain non-cleared despite the repaired conditional.
2. **Action distinctness:** on this exact scenario, does `pub1` direct respect/deferral while revised `pub2` directs an affirmative recommendation for timely implantation?
3. **Axis spanning:** do the public and framework layers now occupy both action orientations without stereotyping Deaf versus hearing families?
4. **Scenario/action divergence:** are both public candidates applicable to the same stipulated parents and facts?

The repair should be accepted only if gate 1 survives the stricter Full Corpus source-to-policy rule. The author does **not** assume that removal of the conditional alone is sufficient for clearance.
