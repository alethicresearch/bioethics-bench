## M169 — solar-geoengineering field research

M169 was the only case in this batch that looked plausibly executable after deep research. It does **not** survive the stricter candidate audit.

The public evidence is relevant and substantial: nationally representative and multinational studies show heterogeneous support, risk perceptions, trust and governance preferences around solar geoengineering and related climate interventions. However, those studies do not directly support two action-distinct institutional rules for the bounded experiment represented here, such as:

1. authorize this small outdoor substance-release experiment under robust governance; versus
2. refuse authorization until a stronger international or affected-region decision mechanism exists.

Translating generalized support, concern or demand for engagement into those two authorization rules would recreate the older Featured editorial-translation standard. Under the Full Corpus rule, the evidence must already support the core action/direction.

The National Academies source does directly support one professional governance architecture: a transparent, permitted, independently reviewed research program in which outdoor experiments involving substance release are justified case by case and used only when critical observations cannot otherwise be obtained. It does not supply a second professional policy.

**Disposition:** `needs-additional-evidence`. Retain as a major planetary-health governance case. Re-open if deliberative-public or affected-region research directly compares authorization/defer/consent rules for outdoor SRM experiments.

## M170 — genetically modified mosquito release

Community studies show meaningful conditional support and concerns about trust, control, reversibility and local process. WHO/TDR supplies a mature staged testing, risk-benefit, regulatory and engagement architecture. The community evidence does not yet directly divide into two field-release policies under the strict rule.

**Disposition:** `needs-additional-evidence`.
