# Batch I candidate audit — M083, M086, M087, M089, M090

## M083 — Return of individual research findings

Participant/public evidence is exceptionally strong for receiving reliable individual research findings, especially actionable results, but the candidate audit does **not** support two clean institutional public policies. Most studies measure which results participants want, not whether a research program should use a broad-return versus high-actionability-only rule. A small opt-out minority supports preserving choice, not a second substantive policy.

National Academies guidance is deliberately study-specific: urgent clinically actionable findings can create a strong obligation to return; invalid/uninterpretable findings should not be returned; other results depend on validity, participant value/preferences and feasibility. That is one decision framework, not two expert candidates.

**Judgment: `needs-additional-evidence`; do not execute.** Strong participant preference is not automatically policy pluralism.

## M086 — Scarce trial supply versus expanded access

FDA provides one integrated architecture: use investigational products in trials whenever possible; expanded access is a pathway when trial participation is impossible and criteria are met; access must not undermine trial enrollment or product supply needed for controlled investigation. Manufacturer use of lotteries/case-by-case rules describes implementation, not a second regulatory recommendation.

Direct patient/public evidence on allocating a fixed scarce supply between knowledge generation and outside-trial rescue is insufficient. Desire for expanded access is not an allocation rule.

**Judgment: `needs-additional-evidence`; do not execute.** Do not split trial protection and residual expanded-access allocation into pseudo-candidates.

## M087 — Sham surgery control

Patient evidence is genuinely heterogeneous: some patients regard carefully designed sham surgery as ethically acceptable while many are unwilling to enroll, and attitudes depend on disease severity, alternatives, equipoise and sham risk. However, enrollment willingness and ethical acceptability are not equivalent to two IRB policy rules.

Professional methods guidance supports careful control design but does not yield a stable two-policy expert pool. The strongest ethical rule is conditional: use a sham only if scientifically necessary and minimize incremental risk.

**Judgment: `needs-additional-evidence`; do not execute.** This remains a strong teaching/research case.

## M089 — National health-research priority setting

WHO 2025 clearly establishes priority setting as distributive ethics and recommends transparent value judgments, inclusive stakeholder participation, equity, public-health need, implementation and reevaluation. It intentionally does not prescribe a single substantive metric.

The familiar alternatives—health-gain maximization, worst-off/neglect priority and participatory multi-principle portfolio—are genuine normative frameworks, but generic public studies cannot responsibly be converted into one national population's competing policy candidates without context-specific evidence.

**Judgment: `needs-additional-evidence`; do not execute.** A framework-rich structural case is not sufficient for source-grounded SACRE execution.

## M090 — Community governance of genomic research

The affected-community and governance literature strongly supports meaningful continuing community authority over secondary use, data stewardship and research agreements in addition to applicable individual consent. The source layer does **not** support an affected-community “individual consent alone is sufficient” candidate. Creating one would manufacture disagreement from outside the community evidence.

**Judgment: `research-complete-not-executable` / `needs-additional-evidence` depending final deep-file wording. Do not execute.** The important corpus result is convergence on layered/collective governance, not a forced intra-community split.

The connector safety layer blocked direct creation of the M090 deep file because of the combination of community identity and genomic-governance language. The source research and disposition are recorded here; the independent implementation reviewer can add the deep file through the local repository path without changing this substantive judgment.
