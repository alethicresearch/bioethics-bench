# M174 — International allocation of climate-health adaptation finance

**Domain:** climate justice / global health finance  
**Evidence date:** 2026-08-27  
**Research status:** deep case draft

## Decision
**Decision-maker:** international climate-health fund.  
**Question:** How should limited adaptation finance be allocated among countries differing in climate vulnerability, historical emissions, population, projected health impact and implementation capacity?

## Scenario
The fund has more eligible health-adaptation proposals than resources. Country A is extremely climate-vulnerable with low historical emissions but weaker implementation capacity. Country B has a large population and a project with the greatest modeled health benefit per dollar. Country C has high vulnerability and stronger institutional capacity but benefited historically from emissions-intensive development. The fund must choose whether to prioritize vulnerability/need, responsibility and historical contribution, expected health impact/absorptive capacity, or use an explicit multiprinciple formula.

## Evidence layers
The Green Climate Fund already uses a multiprinciple architecture: impact potential, paradigm shift, sustainable development, recipient need, country ownership, and efficiency/effectiveness, while preserving substantial adaptation funding for particularly vulnerable countries. This makes pure single-principle alternatives useful philosophical comparators but not coequal professional policies.

## Candidate families
1. vulnerability/need priority;
2. responsibility/historical-contribution priority;
3. expected health impact/implementation capacity;
4. multiprinciple allocation formula.

## Risks
Do not treat implementation capacity as moral desert or historical emissions as the only justice consideration. Distinguish allocation rules from eligibility criteria.

## Executable judgment
`research-complete-not-executable`. Professional governance already integrates multiple values; direct affected-country preference evidence does not support a clean alternative pool.
