# Bioethics Bench — Master Case Inventory

**Status:** research inventory, not a frozen benchmark release  
**Purpose:** maintain an open-ended, source-grounded universe of practical-ethics decision problems from which Featured, teaching, demonstration, development, stress-test and later empirical collections can be constructed.

The master inventory is intentionally **not capped**. A good research case remains useful even if it is not used in the first Featured release. The public Featured Collection is therefore a curated, versioned slice of this inventory rather than the entire research universe.

## The key distinction

1. **Master inventory** — open-ended research map. Topics may have many position families and extensive source material.
2. **Deep case file** — case-specific literature, factual assumptions, public evidence, professional positions, framework derivations and editorial decisions.
3. **Executable case record** — compact, versioned, reviewed scenario and candidate set designed for repeatable SACRE/QCCS execution and matched human comparison.
4. **Featured release** — a public, browsable set of reviewed executable cases. Releases can grow over time; there is no permanent 24–30-case ceiling.

A topic being important does not make it benchmark-ready. A case must still survive the eligibility and construction requirements.

## How to read the inventory

Each entry records:

- **Decision** — the bounded question an eventual scenario would put to a represented decision-maker;
- **Position families** — substantive policy families supported by the literature, not yet the final executable candidate texts;
- **Research anchors / casebooks** — source families that make the topic researchable;
- **Use** — `C` canonical/core, `M` methodologically informative, `F` frontier/contemporary;
- **Construction issue** — the main problem to solve before neutral executable drafting.

`PSAI` marks topics also represented in the Practical Ethics course repository (`alethicresearch/psai`). The course is used as a map of established practical-ethics questions, not as empirical evidence of a public/expert position.

`LEGACY:<id>` marks an underlying topic from the historical pre-clean SACRE examples. Historical wording/results remain historical; only the decision problem is being reconsidered.

## Discovery streams

The inventory intentionally combines four different sampling frames:

- **casebooks and curricula** — recurring, pedagogically legible and everyday dilemmas;
- **professional guidance and policy** — actual institutional decision architectures;
- **normative and empirical bioethics literature** — arguments, concepts and documented public/professional views;
- **emerging governance** — newly actionable questions in AI, genomics, neurotechnology, climate/health, One Health, biosecurity and related areas.

No one stream defines bioethics. Their overlap is useful; their disagreement is also useful.

## Research rules for moving an inventory topic into a case record

Before drafting an executable record:

1. identify the actual decision-maker and singular decision;
2. assemble case-specific primary/professional sources plus serious normative literature;
3. identify public-preference evidence where it exists, without inventing a public consensus where it does not;
4. identify professional/expert policy positions separately from empirical facts;
5. identify framework-derived recommendations and record the reasoning bridge from principle to policy;
6. specify jurisdiction/time only when necessary and version it when those facts change;
7. state outcome-critical factual uncertainty rather than hiding it;
8. reconstruct the scenario independently rather than copying casebook or article narrative;
9. construct the executable case under `CASE_CONSTRUCTION_STANDARD.md`;
10. independently review scenario and candidate provenance before public release.

The master inventory is intentionally permissive. The executable-record standard is intentionally strict.
