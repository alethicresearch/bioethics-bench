# Featured v1 — source and provenance ledger

**Status:** working research ledger, 26 August 2026.  
**Purpose:** distinguish evidence that actually supports a represented source-pool position from background material that merely explains the ethical problem. A source being publicly accessible does **not** make it `public` evidence.

## Evidence labels

- **A — direct:** empirical survey, deliberation, consultation, participant/community study, or explicit position from the relevant source class supports the represented recommendation or a very close policy family.
- **B — indirect/interpretive:** evidence directly supports the values, concerns, or process preference, but editorial translation is required to make it an actionable policy candidate.
- **C — insufficient for freeze:** useful background, but not enough to call the executable candidate source-derived. Must be strengthened or the candidate rewritten before release.

No Featured record is released with a `public` candidate at C.

# Immediate candidate revisions implied by the source pass

Keep `pub1` as parent-choice/shared decision. Revise `pub2` so it does **not** imply parents want a rigid institutional age default. The evidence supports a second family in which parents want clinicians to help interpret prognosis and, when desired, provide a recommendation while preserving parental authority in the gray zone.

## F06

Keep a conditional low-risk placebo candidate. Revise the second public candidate from a categorical active-control requirement to a preference for active/add-on control where an effective therapy exists and placebo would create meaningful treatment deprivation, reflecting trial-participant reluctance and conditional public acceptance.

## F07

Rewrite public candidates around evidence actually produced by community processes:

1. a standard-of-care package co-designed with participant/community representatives and not justified merely by local deprivation;
2. a locally relevant design accompanied by sustainable benefits/access and a mechanism for community ownership and ongoing revision.

The stronger `best global proven intervention` presumption belongs in the expert/framework pools.

## F08

The public layer is no longer weak. Use Yui et al. 2023 and IVF/ICSI patient work directly to represent **caution/retain the boundary** versus **conditional extension under public legitimacy and specialized oversight**. Do not use the pending Nuffield dialogue as if it already returned a verdict.

## F17

Public evidence directly supports the conditional structure already used: medical animal research is more acceptable where purpose is serious and no adequate alternative exists; the public also shows strong interest in replacement and welfare. Keep animals' direct moral status out of the `public` provenance and in framework/expert reasoning.

## F18

The current candidates are well aligned with consumer evidence. Preserve the distinction between unnecessary/routine use and clinically necessary treatment; a no-antibiotics absolutist policy would misrepresent both public evidence and animal-welfare obligations.

# Freeze rule for `public` provenance

For each executable public candidate the future JSON record should state one of:

- `extracted-from-evidence` — the source directly asks or deliberates on a closely matching policy;
- `adapted-from-source` — the source establishes a coherent preference/value family and the Bench converts it into an actionable policy;
- `editorial` — allowed only during development and **not acceptable for a released Featured public candidate** unless the label/pool design itself is explicitly changed.

The Bench must never imply that an editor's plausible intuition is an empirical public preference.
