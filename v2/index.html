What changed

From “benchmark dataset” to inspectable research infrastructure.

The current Bench is not a table of moral answers. It is a structured substrate for studying how represented normative positions relate to one another, how those relations change under different representations and evaluators, and how provenance and review state affect what an evaluation can legitimately claim.

Why the corpus is not a balanced grid

Candidate geometry follows evidence, not cosmetic symmetry.

The 34 executable families occupy eight distinct Public × Expert × Framework geometries. Only eight families use a symmetric 2×2×2 shape; the other 26 retain evidence-qualified asymmetric candidate sets. Under the first SACRE/QCCS task, that geometry produces 428 unordered cross-source comparisons for one representation and 856 for one matched concise+detailed pass.

Source-grounded candidates

Public, expert and framework pools are task-facing roles, while candidate provenance records how each policy was constructed and what evidence relationship supports it.

Controlled representations

Concise and detailed companions hold the decision question, candidate set and stipulations fixed while varying scenario detail. Representation is therefore an experimental axis rather than an uncontrolled rewrite.

Explicit benchmark assumptions

Stipulations make otherwise underdetermined decisions executable enough to study. They are declared measurement controls, not empirical findings about the world.

Results belong to a different layer

Explore results without turning them into an answer key.

The v2 results view separates two kinds of evidence: a completed reproducibility result about the resource→task projection, and bounded developmental model runs used to study representation and stability. Neither establishes moral truth, QCCS validity or human correspondence.

Review and provenance

The source relationship is part of the scientific object.

Corpus review showed that “has a citation” is not enough. A defensible normative resource needs to distinguish the source itself from what it is doing in the argument and from the policy translation the Bench makes from it.

What remains deliberately unfinished

Independent corpus-wide human source review, an implemented generic v2 schema, a mature second task family, QCCS reliability/construct validity and human-model correspondence remain future work. The website should make those boundaries legible rather than smoothing them away.

Research use

Every claim should identify the resource, the task and the evaluation condition.

That separation is the practical reproducibility rule behind the project. A paper-facing result should be reconstructable to the exact record/version/hash, task protocol and evaluator/configuration that produced it.
