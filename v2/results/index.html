Explore what has been demonstrated — and what has not.

This page separates a completed reproducibility result about the Bench→SACRE task projection from bounded developmental model executions. The latter are useful for studying representation and stability, but they are not confirmatory validation, a model leaderboard or evidence that a top-ranked policy is morally correct.

Repeat calibration

Stability can differ sharply by case and representation.

Selected families were repeated four or five times to estimate whether the top-two ranking gap was large relative to ordinary run-to-run score variation. This was hypothesis-generating calibration, not a preregistered reliability study.

Current evidence ceiling

What these results can and cannot establish.

Supported now

Exact resource/task separation; reproducible sacre-qccs-v1 projection across all 68 records; developmental evidence that representation and repeated execution can alter pair scores, rankings and sometimes the provisional top policy; operational completion/failure observations.

Not established

QCCS reliability or construct validity, corpus-wide source fidelity, human-model correspondence, moral correctness, model superiority, method-neutrality across multiple mature tasks, or confirmatory P3 validation.
